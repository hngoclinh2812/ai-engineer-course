You: What is tropical climate?
Claude: A tropical climate is a climate found in regions near the equator, generally between the Tropic of Cancer and the Tropic of Capricorn. Its main features are:

- **Warm temperatures year-round:** Average monthly temperatures are usually above about 18°C (64°F), and there is little seasonal variation. The difference between summer and winter is small.
- **High humidity:** The air holds a lot of moisture, so it often feels muggy.
- **Abundant rainfall:** Many tropical areas get heavy rain, often concentrated in a wet season, though some receive less.
- **Strong sunlight:** The sun is high in the sky for much of the year, so solar radiation is intense.

Tropical climates are often divided into subtypes based on rainfall:

- **Tropical rainforest:** Rain falls throughout the year, with no real dry season.
- **Tropical monsoon:** Has a short dry season and a heavy wet season, usually driven by seasonal wind shifts.
- **Tropical savanna:** Has a distinct long dry season and a wet season.

Ho Chi Minh City falls into the tropical savanna or tropical monsoon category, with its dry season from December to April and its rainy season from May to November.
